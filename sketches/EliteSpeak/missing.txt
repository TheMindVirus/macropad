DMX State Recall:
* A safety measure used to hold a temporary automation state while switching over console equipment
* Works with DMX512 RS485 Compatible Systems by utilising the usually resistor terminated RDM channel
* DMX is Digital MultiPlex and RDM is Remote Device Management where one calls out and the other responds
* The RDM response is often unstable for use in live production and is switched off most of the time
* Once it works it would be able to parity check whether outgoing data bytes are the same as incoming
* If there is a difference between outgoing data and incoming data then the line may need repairing
* Also may work with other Recommended Standards that require an additional return duplex or triplex
* Signal carried over 3-Pin XLR and originally twice over on 5-Pin XLR with some opting for 8-Pin DIN
