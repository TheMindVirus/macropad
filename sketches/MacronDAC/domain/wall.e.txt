# Macron Domain Potential Encodings:
0 - No Change
1 - Macron
2 - Micron
3 - Macron + Micron #!!! Micron + Macron prints differently

0 - Macron + Micron
1 - Micron
2 - Macron
3 - No Change

Either way, the encoding follows a big.LITTLE type structure
where 0 being No Change is LITTLE and 3 being No Change is big
      - ----
e.g. +130.195
       --   -
Optional dashes may be added to the decimal point and/or sign
to indicate which way the decimal point moved during alignment
